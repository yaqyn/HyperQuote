Lyon is HyperQuote's AI assistant, available across the platform as a conversational interface for finding materials, checking order status, building material lists, and getting instant answers to common questions. It understands both Arabic and English and adapts its capabilities based on where you are in the platform.

## What Lyon can help with

On the **website**, Lyon operates as a guided discovery tool. It answers questions about products, helps you navigate the catalog, and captures your interest as a potential lead. Ask "What types of cement do you carry?" or "Do you deliver to 6th of October City?" and Lyon responds with relevant information and next steps.

In the **customer portal**, Lyon becomes your primary interaction point. Its capabilities expand significantly:

- **Material ordering** — "I need 400 bags of OPC 50kg cement" translates into a structured material list ready for your review. No forms, no product codes — just describe what you need in plain language.
- **Status lookups** — "Where is my delivery?" or "What is the status of order HQ-2026-0412?" returns real-time information pulled from the order tracking system.
- **Reordering** — "Reorder what I got last month" pulls your previous order, creates an editable draft, and asks for confirmation before submitting.
- **General questions** — "What are your payment terms?" or "How does the quoting process work?" returns accurate, contextual answers.

## How to use Lyon

Lyon appears as a floating chat button on the website and as the centered primary interface in the customer portal. Tap or click to start a conversation. Type in Arabic or English — Lyon detects the language and responds in kind.

Every action Lyon takes produces an **editable draft**. When Lyon parses "I need 500 units of 12mm rebar and 200 bags of cement," it creates a material list that you review, adjust, and confirm before anything is submitted. Lyon never places an order or sends a quote request without your explicit approval.

Conversations are preserved across sessions. You can return to a previous discussion, reference past requests, and build on earlier work.

## AI estimation tool

Lyon includes a project estimation capability. Describe a project — "Building a 3-floor apartment, 200 square meters per floor" — and Lyon researches typical material requirements, generates an estimated material list with quantities, and presents it for your review.

This estimation tool carries clear disclaimers: "Verify with your engineer" appears on every AI-generated estimate. Lyon frames these outputs as starting points for discussion, not as engineering specifications. The estimates are editable — adjust quantities, swap materials, add or remove items before submitting as a quote request.

## What Lyon does not do

Lyon does not access your payment information, modify confirmed orders, or make purchasing decisions on your behalf. All mutations require your confirmation. Lyon cannot override pricing, negotiate on your behalf, or access other customers' data.

Behind the scenes, Lyon routes queries through a multi-tier AI system that uses the most appropriate model for each request: lightweight models for simple lookups and classification, faster models for conversational responses in Arabic, and more capable models for complex reasoning like project estimation. This keeps responses fast while maintaining quality where it matters.
