Browsing the HyperQuote catalog requires no account and no login. You can explore the full range of building materials as a guest, review specifications, and assess availability before deciding to request a quote.

## Catalog structure

Products are organized into categories that reflect how construction professionals actually buy: cement and concrete, steel and rebar, lumber and timber, roofing materials, plumbing and pipes, electrical, hardware and fixtures, and specialty items. Each category contains subcategories with filters for brand, material grade, dimensions, and other relevant specifications.

Every product listing shows the product name in both Arabic and English, along with the manufacturer, key specifications, and available documentation like datasheets and compliance certificates.

## Price ranges, not fixed prices

HyperQuote operates on a quote-based model, so you will not see exact prices in the catalog. Instead, product listings display **price ranges** — for example, "Starting from EGP 47/bag for 100+ bags." These directional signals help you estimate project costs before submitting a formal quote request.

This approach exists because building material prices in Egypt fluctuate daily based on supply, demand, and supplier relationships. A fixed published price would be stale within hours for volatile commodities like steel and cement. The price range gives you enough information to plan without creating false expectations.

## Availability indicators

Each product shows one of five availability tiers based on aggregated supplier stock data:

- **Available** — Suppliers report adequate stock and data is fresh
- **Low Stock** — Stock is below normal thresholds or data is aging
- **Available on Request** — Stock data requires confirmation from the supplier
- **Lead Time: X days** — Made-to-order or long-lead items with estimated timelines
- **Out of Stock** — Confirmed unavailable from current suppliers

These indicators reflect supplier-reported inventory across HyperQuote's network. The actual quantities available for your order are confirmed during the quoting process, when suppliers commit specific stock against your request.

## Browsing without an account

Guest visitors can browse the full catalog, view all product specifications and datasheets, and use search and filters freely. Registration is only required when you are ready to submit a quote request — and even then, the first stage requires just four fields: phone number, company name, full name, and OTP verification.

## Adding items to a quote

While browsing, you can add any product to a quote request using the "Add to Quote" button on product cards and detail pages. These selections accumulate in your material list, which you can review, adjust quantities, and organize by project before submitting as a formal RFQ.

If you prefer, you can skip the catalog entirely and use the AI assistant to describe what you need in plain language. The assistant will identify the right products and draft a material list for your review.
