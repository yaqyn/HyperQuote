## English

### Who Sends Messages

**HyperQuote** uses WhatsApp as a communication channel to send you messages related to your use of our B2B building materials logistics platform. All WhatsApp messages are sent by HyperQuote through the Meta WhatsApp Business API.

### Communication Channel

You will receive messages on **WhatsApp** at the phone number you provide during registration or update in your account settings.

### Types of Messages

HyperQuote may send you the following types of messages via WhatsApp:

| Message Type | Description | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Order Confirmations** | Notification that your order has been received, confirmed, or updated | User places or modifies an order |
| **Delivery Status Updates** | Real-time updates on delivery progress, dispatch, en-route, arrival, and completion | Delivery status changes |
| **Payment Notifications** | Payment receipts, payment due reminders, credit updates, and wallet balance changes | Payment events |
| **Support Responses** | Replies to your support inquiries and ticket updates | Support team responds |
| **Account Alerts** | Security notifications, account changes, and system announcements | Security or account events |

**Note:** HyperQuote does not send unsolicited marketing or promotional messages via WhatsApp without your explicit, separate consent for marketing communications.

### How You Opt In

You opt in to receive WhatsApp messages from HyperQuote through one of the following mechanisms:

1. **During registration**: By checking the WhatsApp communication checkbox on the registration form, you consent to receive transactional WhatsApp messages from HyperQuote
2. **First WhatsApp interaction**: If you initiate a WhatsApp conversation with HyperQuote, you consent to receive responses and related transactional messages through the same channel

Your opt-in is recorded with a timestamp for compliance purposes.

### How You Opt Out

You may opt out of WhatsApp messages at any time through any of the following methods:

1. **Reply "STOP"**: Reply with the word **STOP** to any WhatsApp message from HyperQuote. You will receive a confirmation that you have been unsubscribed
2. **Account settings**: Disable WhatsApp notifications in your account notification preferences
3. **Contact support**: Request opt-out by emailing support@hyperquote.net

Opting out of WhatsApp messages does not affect your ability to use the HyperQuote platform. You will continue to receive important communications via email and in-app notifications.

### Message Frequency

- **Transactional messages** are triggered by your actions on the platform (placing orders, making payments, etc.). Frequency depends on your usage
- HyperQuote does not send messages at excessive frequencies
- You will not receive messages outside of reasonable business hours unless they are time-sensitive delivery or security alerts

### Data

- Your phone number is stored in our system for the purpose of sending WhatsApp messages
- Message history is retained in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy-en.md) retention schedule (1 year after account closure)
- Your phone number will not be shared with third parties for messaging purposes

### Contact

For questions about WhatsApp messaging:
Email: support@hyperquote.net
