## 1. Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to all employees, contractors, and authorized personnel ("Staff") who access HyperQuote internal tools, including but not limited to:

- **HQE** (HyperQuote Employee Platform)
- **HQAdmin** (Administration Dashboard)
- **HQCommand** (CEO Command Center)
- Any other internal systems, tools, or services provided by HyperQuote

This policy supplements, but does not replace, your employment contract and any applicable confidentiality agreements.

---

## 2. Acceptable Use

HyperQuote internal tools are provided for **business purposes only**. You must:

- Use internal systems solely for authorized work activities
- Follow established workflows and procedures
- Report system issues or errors through proper channels
- Cooperate with IT and security teams when requested

---

## 3. Data Confidentiality

You have access to sensitive business data, including customer information, supplier details, financial records, and operational data. You must:

- Treat all customer and supplier data as **confidential**
- Never share customer or supplier information with unauthorized parties
- Follow the company's data classification policy
- Never export customer or business data to personal devices, personal email accounts, or unauthorized cloud services
- Never share screenshots or exports of internal dashboards externally

---

## 4. Account Security

- Your account credentials are **personal and non-transferable**
- Never share your password or access tokens with anyone, including colleagues
- Use strong passwords and enable multi-factor authentication when available
- Lock your workstation when unattended
- Report any suspected security breach or unauthorized access **immediately** to IT security at security@hyperquote.net

---

## 5. System Monitoring

HyperQuote may monitor usage of internal systems for:

- Security and fraud detection
- Compliance with company policies
- System performance and reliability
- Audit trail requirements under Egyptian law

All monitoring is conducted in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy-en.md) and applicable Egyptian labor and data protection laws.

---

## 6. Prohibited Activities

The following are strictly prohibited:

- Accessing data or systems beyond your authorized role and permissions
- Attempting to circumvent security controls or access restrictions
- Installing unauthorized software on company devices
- Using internal tools for personal business or non-work activities
- Sharing confidential business information on social media or public forums
- Modifying or deleting records without proper authorization

---

## 7. Consequences

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Verbal or written warning
- Temporary suspension of system access
- Disciplinary action per your employment contract
- Termination of employment in accordance with Egyptian Labor Law No. 12 of 2003
- Legal action for serious violations (data theft, fraud, unauthorized disclosure)

---

## 8. Acknowledgment

By using HyperQuote internal systems, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to comply with this Acceptable Use Policy.
