## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to the following minimum retention periods:

| Data Category | Retention Period | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| **Financial records** (invoices, payments, journal entries, tax documents) | **5 years** from the date of the transaction | Egyptian tax law requirements |
| **Personal data** (name, email, phone, address) | **1 year** after account closure or last activity | Contractual and legal follow-up |
| **System logs** (access logs, technical data, audit trails) | **90 days** from creation | Security monitoring and troubleshooting |
| **Communication data** (WhatsApp messages, support tickets) | Per privacy policy retention schedule — **1 year** after account closure | Service continuity and dispute resolution |

After the applicable retention period expires, data is securely deleted or anonymized. Financial data subject to legal hold or ongoing disputes may be retained beyond the standard period as required by law.

## 6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We share your personal data with the following categories of third parties, only to the extent necessary:

### 6.1 Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA)

We are legally required to submit electronic invoices to the Egyptian Tax Authority for all B2B transactions. This includes invoice details, buyer and seller tax IDs, item descriptions, quantities, prices, and tax amounts. This is a legal obligation under Egyptian tax law and is not optional. See our [ETA E-Invoice Disclosure](./eta-disclosure.md) for details.

### 6.2 Payment Processors

We share transaction data with payment processors to facilitate payments. Data shared is limited to what is necessary to process the transaction.

### 6.3 Delivery Partners

We share limited operational data with delivery personnel and logistics partners, including delivery addresses, contact information for the delivery recipient, and order details necessary for fulfillment.

### 6.4 Infrastructure Providers

- **Cloudflare**: Our platform runs on Cloudflare's global edge network. Data may be processed at Cloudflare edge locations worldwide as part of content delivery and security services.
- **Supabase**: Our database infrastructure is hosted on Supabase cloud services. Your data is stored in Supabase-managed databases.

### 6.5 No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 9. Your Rights Under the PDPL

As a data subject, you have the following rights under the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law:

1. **Right of Access**: You may request access to the personal data we hold about you
2. **Right of Rectification**: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
3. **Right of Erasure**: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to limitations for data we are legally required to retain (e.g., financial records for 5 years)
4. **Right of Restriction**: You may request that we limit the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances
5. **Right to Data Portability**: You may request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
6. **Right to Object**: You may object to the processing of your personal data for specific purposes, including direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: **data-protection@hyperquote.net**

We will respond to your request within 30 days. If we cannot fulfill your request (e.g., due to legal obligations), we will explain the reasons.
