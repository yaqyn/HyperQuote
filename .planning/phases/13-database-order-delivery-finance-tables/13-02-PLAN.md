**Procurement tables (Section 3.7):**
1. `supplier_pos` — Full SQL in 13-CONTEXT.md lines 209-249. FK to orders(id), suppliers(id). `receiving_warehouse_id` column WITHOUT FK here (added at bottom after warehouses). ~35 columns.
2. `supplier_po_items` — Full SQL in 13-CONTEXT.md lines 253-270. FK to supplier_pos(id) ON DELETE CASCADE, order_items(id), products(id).
3. `supplier_inquiries` — Full SQL in 13-CONTEXT.md lines 273-291. FK to quote_requests(id), suppliers(id). items/response_items as JSONB.

**Inventory tables (Section 3.8, in FK order):**
4. `warehouses` — tenant_id, name, code (UNIQUE with tenant_id), address fields, is_active.
5. `warehouse_locations` — FK to warehouses(id). zone, aisle, rack, shelf, bin. UNIQUE (warehouse_id, zone, aisle, rack, shelf, bin).
6. `inventory` — FK to products(id), warehouses(id), warehouse_locations(id). GENERATED ALWAYS AS columns:
   - `quantity_available DECIMAL(12,3) GENERATED ALWAYS AS (quantity_on_hand - quantity_reserved - quantity_allocated) STORED`
   - `atp DECIMAL(12,3) GENERATED ALWAYS AS (quantity_on_hand - quantity_reserved - quantity_allocated + quantity_incoming) STORED`
   UNIQUE constraint with COALESCE: `UNIQUE (tenant_id, product_id, warehouse_id, COALESCE(location_id, '00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000'), COALESCE(lot_number, ''))`
7. `stock_movements` — FK to inventory(id), products(id), warehouses(id). movement_type, quantity, reference fields.
8. `inventory_transfers` — FK to warehouses (from/to). transfer_number, status, items JSONB.
9. `cycle_counts` — FK to warehouses(id). count_number, status, counted_by.
10. `source_inventory` — FK to products(id), suppliers(id), warehouses(id). supplier-owned stock tracking.
11. `inventory_reservations` — FK to inventory(id), orders(id). reserved_quantity, expiry, status.

```sql
END $$;
```

Add triggers: set_tenant_id and update_updated_at on all tables with those columns.
