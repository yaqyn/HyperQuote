     * `vehicle_inspections`: vehicle_id, driver_id, inspection_type (pre_trip/post_trip), status (in_progress/completed/failed), odometer_reading (real), signature_url, gps_lat (real), gps_lng (real), notes, created_at

     * `deliveries`: order_id, route_id, driver_id, customer_id, status, scheduled_date, actual_arrival, actual_departure, pod_signature_url, pod_photos, notes
     * `delivery_items`: delivery_id, product_id, product_name, quantity_expected (real), quantity_delivered (real), unit, status, damage_notes, photo_url
     * `routes`: driver_id, vehicle_id, date, status, total_distance_km (real), total_weight_kg (real), estimated_finish, actual_finish, stop_count (integer)
     * `route_stops`: route_id, delivery_id, stop_order (integer), customer_name, address, lat (real), lng (real), status, eta, notes, unloading_method
   - Create `DriverSchema` from `new Schema({...})`

   - Export `SupabaseConnector` class
