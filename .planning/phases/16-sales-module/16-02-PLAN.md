    **TierBadge.tsx** -- Displays customer tier:
    - Props: `tier: 'A' | 'B' | 'C' | 'new'`
    - Renders a small pill/badge. Tier A: blue bg, Tier B: neutral bg, Tier C: light bg, New: dashed border.
    - Three-color compliant: use opacity variants of existing colors only (white/black/blue).
    - Text in Geist Mono if showing tier letter.

    **AgeTimer.tsx** -- Real-time aging timer (Geist Mono):
    - Props: `createdAt: string` (ISO date)
    - Uses `useEffect` + `setInterval(1000)` to tick elapsed time.
    - Color transitions: green (<2h) -> yellow (2-4h) -> orange (4-8h) -> red (>8h). Pulsing red at >24h (use CSS `animate-pulse`).
    - Format: "Xh Ym" or "Xm Ys" if under 1 hour.
    - All numbers in `font-[family-name:var(--font-geist-mono)]`.
    - NOTE: This is raw elapsed time, NOT business-hours adjusted. Business hour adjustment happens in SLACountdown.

    **SLACountdown.tsx** -- SLA countdown timer:
    - Props: `deadline: string` (ISO date), `tier: string`
    - Shows time remaining until SLA deadline. Counts DOWN, not up.
    - Color: green (>50% remaining) -> yellow (25-50%) -> red (<25%) -> pulsing red (overdue).
    - Accounts for Egyptian business hours (Sun-Thu, configurable hours). Uses a `calculateBusinessTimeRemaining(deadline: string, now: Date): number` helper that skips Fri-Sat.
    - Format: "Xh Ym remaining" or "Xh Ym overdue" (red).
    - All numbers Geist Mono.

    **CreditStatusBanner.tsx** -- Yellow banner for credit status:
    - Props: `creditLimit: number, currentExposure: number, availableCredit: number`
    - Shows: "Customer credit: EGP {{available}} available of EGP {{limit}} limit"
    - Yellow background when available < 30% of limit.
    - Red background when available <= 0.
    - All amounts in Geist Mono with `Intl.NumberFormat('ar-EG')` for Arabic locale.
    - Includes "Request Credit Limit Increase" button (logs action, not wired to real flow).
